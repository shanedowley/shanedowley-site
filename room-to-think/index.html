Room to Think

What is Room to Think?

Room to Think is a confidential, focused professional coaching conversation for managers, leaders, executives and entrepreneurs.

Bring an issue, challenge or decision that’s taking up too much of your thinking. We’ll spend up to one hour working through it together. We’ll create the space for it, look at it from a range of perspectives and deepen our understanding of what’s really going on. From there, we’ll work together to design a way forward.

This is a one-off conversation. There’s no programme, package or ongoing commitment.

What could you leave with?

A clearer understanding of what’s really going on.

A different perspective on the situation.

Greater clarity about the choices available to you.

Practical actions that can help you move forward.

Clarity creates choice.

How it works

Before

Once you’ve booked, you’ll have the option to answer three short questions to help frame what you’d like to bring. A few sentences is plenty.

During

We’ll have a focused, confidential coaching conversation of up to 60 minutes. You bring the issue. We’ll work with whatever emerges and matters most.

After

I’ll send you a concise, personalised Action Summary capturing what became clearer, the choices you see and the actions you identified.

And that’s it. No ongoing commitment.

Why Shane?

Shane understands what it’s like to carry responsibility in complex, fast-moving organisations. He’s spent much of his career leading significant programmes and projects where decisions matter, priorities compete and progress depends on working effectively with other people.

Through that experience, he learned the value of remaining grounded under pressure — being able to stop, step back, reassess what’s happening, see the situation from a different perspective and choose how to move forward.

And sometimes we need to do that, fast.

Today, his work as an executive coach, facilitator and organisational consultant builds on that experience — helping other leaders create enough space within the complexity to see more clearly, choose deliberately and act.

A cool head. Another perspective. Room to think.

A few practical questions

Is Room to Think ongoing coaching?

No. Room to Think is deliberately a one-off conversation. There’s no programme, package or ongoing commitment.

What if we don’t need the full hour?

That’s fine. Your Room to Think is booked for up to 60 minutes, but there’s no need to fill the hour if we’ve got where we need to get to.

Is the conversation confidential?

Yes. Room to Think is a confidential professional coaching conversation. I’ll only use what you share to support our conversation and your agreed Action Summary.

Where does the conversation take place?

Online, using Zoom. Your joining details will be included with your booking.

What if I need to change my booking?

You can reschedule with at least 24 hours’ notice. Payments are non-refundable. If you need to change your booking with less than 24 hours’ notice, or miss your appointment, you may reschedule once.
