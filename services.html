Leadership Development

Leadership capability develops through awareness and practice.

Models, tools and frameworks help, but lasting value comes when leaders better understand themselves and those they impact, and apply these insights to their real relationships, decisions and the challenges they face every day.

As an Arbinger Certified Lead Facilitator and Executive Coach, working in association with Arbinger UK, I deliver Outward Mindset programmes that help leaders understand how the way they see others shapes their behaviour, relationships and ultimately their results.

Through licensed programmes and bespoke development, the work spans:

  • accountability and responsibility
  • leadership communication
  • handling conflict and building effective working relationships
  • understanding impact and behaviour
  • working across organisational silos and boundaries
  • performance and supervisory management
  • leading change

The aim is not simply to know more about leadership. It is to lead effectively where it matters.

Executive Coaching

Leadership can be demanding and at times isolating.

The more responsibility we carry as leaders, the fewer opportunities there can be to step outside the pressures of the role and think clearly about what is happening, the choices available to us, and our impact on others.

Working alongside senior leaders, entrepreneurs and professionals carrying significant responsibility, I provide an opportunity to step back, reflect, think and understand.

The coaching is a calm, confidential and at times challenging space to examine the situations you are facing, the assumptions you may be making, and the relationships and behaviours influencing the outcomes you want to achieve.

The work is shaped around you and your context. It involves:

  • strengthening leadership judgement and confidence
  • navigating complexity, uncertainty and change
  • developing greater self-awareness
  • working through difficult relationships or decisions
  • stepping into greater ownership and responsibility
  • understanding your impact on others
  • creating clarity around what matters and what comes next

I will not tell you how to lead. I will help you see more clearly, challenge your thinking and strengthen your ability to make deliberate choices you can stand behind.

Organisational Leadership Consulting

Organisations rarely struggle because people do not care or are not working hard enough.

More often, capable people are operating within conditions that make effective collaboration difficult: competing priorities, unclear outcomes, fragmented information, conflicting incentives, strained relationships and gaps between strategy and everyday work.

I work with leaders and organisations to make those conditions visible and help change them.

We start from where the organisation actually is.

  • What are people experiencing?
  • What is the data showing?
  • Where is alignment weak?
  • Where is progress being blocked?
  • Where has the stated intent become disconnected from what people are actually doing?

From there, we establish a clearer direction and create practical ways to connect organisational outcomes with the work of teams and individuals.

The work includes:

  • organisational alignment
  • defining meaningful outcomes and measures
  • objectives and key results (OKRs)
  • leadership team alignment
  • cross-team collaboration and dependencies
  • organisational and behavioural insight
  • making progress, dependencies and obstacles visible
  • creating feedback that allows the organisation to learn and adapt

I work in association with Stellafai, combining organisational consulting with technology that connects strategic outcomes, goals, measures and organisational progress.

This provides greater visibility into what is happening so leaders and teams can have better conversations, make better-informed decisions and adapt as conditions change.

Clear intent. Meaningful outcomes. Visible progress. People understanding how their contribution connects to the whole.

How we work together

Organisations, situations and people are complex. There is no standard engagement.

Sometimes the right starting point is an initial conversation followed by a series of one-to-one coaching sessions.

Sometimes it is work with a leadership team.

Sometimes the challenge sits across teams, structures and organisational boundaries and requires a wider consulting engagement.

We begin by understanding the situation rather than assuming the solution.

From there, we agree what meaningful progress would look like and the most useful way to work together.
